A note about this publication

Beyond the pages, a world of words.

Beyond The Pages began with a simple thought: writing is more than putting words together. It is a way to express ideas, explore imagination, share perspectives and see the world differently.

This independent, one-writer publication brings together thoughtful essays, informative articles, imaginative short stories and creative pieces. Each piece is written with curiosity and the hope of leaving readers with something to think about, discover or enjoy.

Whether you are here for a new perspective, an interesting article or a quiet moment with a story, I am glad you found your way here.

Aalia C. Founder, Editor & Creator Beyond The Pages
